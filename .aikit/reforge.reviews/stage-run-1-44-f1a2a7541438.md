Boundary: the MAC is only as strong as key custody and authenticates records, not forecast truth; a mixed keyed/unkeyed journal is refused by the write guard (ADR-093); existing unkeyed journals need a re-save to gain MACs; rotation with a key verifies both live and archive first.
